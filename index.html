📖 Quick Rules & Clarifications

Solo Mode Core Rules

  • Game Length: Exactly 4 rounds.
  • Lookout Phase Event: Draw 1 Number Token (1–5) and resolve. If unable to pay cost, lose 2 VP.
  • Scenario Location: Can be activated during the Action Phase. If it's an Action, exhaust it (cover with First Player marker). It does not count for end-of-game VP.
  • Solo Action Tiles: Tiles with pawns flip to the Solo side (higher cost) during Cleanup. Solo tiles without pawns flip back to Standard side.
  • Action Pawns: Can be used twice per round: first placement on tile, then spend 1 Food/Gold to move to an adjacent tile in the circle and exhaust.
  • Goods vs. Resources: Gold substitutes for any 1 resource, but cannot be assigned to cards requiring a named resource. Gold is worth 1 VP at game end.

Expedition Rules

  • Nearby Islands: Can be visited without fish.
  • Distant Islands: Requires 1 Fish assigned to the ship.
  • Pillaging: Gain goods in red banner; discard island.
  • Conquering: Requires 1 Raze token assigned to the ship. Island becomes a location in your Empire (worth 1 VP at end, plus card ability).
